      How to use & shortcuts

      30-second guide

      1. Focus on the injury: pick a Quick view, switch Soft-tissue side to Left or Right, or isolate a body region. Untick Muscles to see ligaments, tendons and nerves underneath.
      2. Find and name structures: point at any part to see its name, click it to open its card, or type a name (such as ACL or rotator) in the search box.
      3. Mark what is involved: pick a highlight colour (red = solid outline, amber = dashed, green = dotted, blue = double outline) or right-click a structure. Tick Fade everything that is not highlighted to make it stand out.
      4. Show or save for the patient: press P for a clean presentation view, or click Save image to download a high-resolution picture for a handout. Nothing is saved or uploaded.
